# Object-only shop illustrations

These 26 editable SVGs and transparent 256x256 PNGs were drawn specifically
for MathGuide on 2026-10-29, in the style of the project's original vector
biscuit, fruit, ball and yarn icons. They are catalog illustrations, not
photographs or extracted frames. They do not contain the pet character.

Rebuild: `python tools/draw_pet_shop_icons.py` (uses local Playwright Chromium).
Each original SVG is retained beside its PNG; `manifest.json` maps the drawing
to the existing interaction clip. No external image files were downloaded or
included in these drawings.

The clips themselves remain the separately licensed upstream animation assets
described in `../../../pet/ASSET-NOTICE.md`. Their original license and
attribution requirements remain in force.
